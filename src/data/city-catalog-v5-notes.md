# Sovereign v5 city catalogue

Prepared 2026-09-09. This is a compact gameplay location catalogue, not a census or a live territorial-control database.

## Coverage and schema

- `city-catalog-v5.json`: 204 country/scenario entity IDs mapped to name, cities, kind, ISO2 and flag. There are 1,198 named management locations.
- All 174 records in the existing `map-data.json` plus Canada are covered (175 starting entities).
- `missing-countries-v5.json`: 29 additional records, including 28 UN members absent from the old map and Holy See/Vatican City. Palestine was already present. This yields all 193 UN members, both UN observer states, and the 9 remaining non-member scenario entities carried over from the old map.
- Added IDs: AND, ATG, BHR, BRB, CPV, COM, DMA, GRD, KIR, LIE, MDV, MHL, MLT, MUS, FSM, MCO, NRU, PLW, KNA, LCA, VCT, WSM, SMR, STP, SYC, SGP, TON, TUV, VAT.
- No record is empty. Names are unique within each entity. Every entity has at least 3 named locations except Vatican City, which has 1. Most entities have 6.
- Added countries have approximate WGS84 lon/lat display anchors, normally in or near their capital/main island. These are not territorial polygons or precise centroids. National and city economic/demographic baselines must be labelled simulated when the engine generates them.
- `iso2` is null for Somaliland and Kosovo. Somaliland has no separate standard emoji. Kosovo uses the widely supported XK emoji, with a field explaining that XK is user-assigned rather than officially assigned ISO 3166-1.
- `kind` distinguishes ordinary cities/settlements, Singapore planning areas, Monaco quarters, TAAF research bases, Vatican city-state, and island settlements.

## Geography and modelling limits

Cities, towns, villages, administrative districts and research bases are all real named places, chosen to provide usable geographic coverage. No fictional fallback locations such as 'Capital district' are included. Familiar major-city names were compiled from established geographic knowledge and the existing city list; uncertain islands, small-state settlements, special districts and bases were spot-checked against the source pages below. This is not a claim that every name was individually verified against a current official gazetteer.

Use neutral 'Locations' or 'Cities and settlements' wording where appropriate. Singapore and Monaco's entries are subdivisions of one city, not independent cities. French Southern and Antarctic Lands has research stations with rotating staff rather than ordinary permanent civilian cities; the listed stations' current operational status is not modelled. Vatican has only one location because inventing cities would be misleading.

The map's country assignment is a scenario simplification. The notes explicitly flag Western Sahara, Somaliland, Kosovo, Cyprus and Moldova/Transnistria. Naming is not an assertion of sovereignty, diplomatic recognition, current military control, or current habitability. In particular, all local populations, output, crime scores, budgets and development indicators generated by the engine must be labelled illustrative gameplay metrics; this catalogue supplies names, not actual crime data.

## Sources consulted

- [GeoNames country codes](https://www.geonames.org/countries/) — ISO2/ISO3 cross-reference and general country geography.
- [About GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/about.html) — database and attribution terms: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Suggested attribution: “Place names spot-checked with GeoNames (CC BY 4.0); curated and simplified for gameplay.”
- [UN non-member observer states](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/non-member-states) — Holy See and Palestine. The UN member-states page returned HTTP 403 during access, so the inventory was cross-checked by ISO country membership with the existing map rather than scraping that page.
- [UN Digital Library: member authorities](https://digitallibrary.un.org/collection/Authorities?ln=en) — 193 member-state count.
- [TAAF: Presentation](https://taaf.fr/collectivites/presentation/) — Port-aux-Français, Alfred-Faure, Martin-de-Viviès research bases.
- [Singapore OneMap: planning areas](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/apidocs/planningarea) — Downtown Core, Tampines, Woodlands, Jurong East, Queenstown and Bedok are planning areas.
- [Singapore URA: Downtown Core](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/urban-design/downtown-core/) — planning-area status.
- [Monaco government: Monaco en chiffres 2022](https://en.gouv.mc/content/download/514984/5896833/file/MAGAZINE_160x240_IMSEE_Monaco%20en%20Chiffres%202022_30-05-2022_interactif_complet.pdf) — named quarters.
- [Falkland Islands government: Our Islands](https://www.falklands.gov.fk/component/jdownloads/?Itemid=101&catid=7&id=36&m=0&task=download.send) — settlement map including Darwin, Goose Green, Port Howard and Fox Bay.
- [Falkland Islands government: Fox Bay](https://falklands.gov.fk/publicworks/services/fox-bay) — settlement name.
- [GeoNames: Nauru populated places](https://www.geonames.org/NR/largest-cities-in-nauru.html) — Arijejen, Denigomodu, Menen, Yangor, Yaren and Anabar.
- [GeoNames: Tuvalu populated places](https://www.geonames.org/TV/largest-cities-in-tuvalu.html) — Funafuti, Savave, Asau, Tanrake, Teava and Kulia.
- [Kiribati National Statistics Office: 2020 census](https://nso.gov.ki/wp-content/uploads/sites/10/wpfd/preview_files/Population-and-Housing-Census-Report-2020%28d46e1fcf4c07ce4a69ee07e4134bcef1%29.pdf) — South Tarawa and Kiritimati village names.
- [GeoNames: Kiribati search](https://www.geonames.org/search.html?country=KI) — Bikenibeu and Tabwakea settlements.
- [GeoNames: Marshall Islands populated places](https://www.geonames.org/MH/largest-cities-in-marshall-islands.html) — Majuro urban settlement, Ebeye (variant Ebaye), Laura, Jabor, Arno, Wotje. The catalogue chooses Delap-Uliga-Djarrit instead of duplicating it under Majuro as a second city.
- [GeoNames: Micronesia populated places](https://www.geonames.org/FM/largest-cities-in-micronesia.html) — Palikir, Weno, Kolonia, Tofol, Colonia, Lelu; Kolonia (Pohnpei) and Colonia (Yap) are separate places, not a spelling duplicate.
- [GeoNames: Palau geography](https://www.geonames.org/countries/PW/palau.html) — Ngerulmud and country codes.
- [GeoNames: Palau populated places](https://www.geonames.org/PW/largest-cities-in-palau.html) — Koror and Kloulklubed.
- [GeoNames: Palau administrative divisions](https://www.geonames.org/PW/administrative-division-palau.html) — Melekeok settlement.
- [GeoNames: Vanuatu populated places](https://www.geonames.org/VU/largest-cities-in-vanuatu.html) — Port Vila, Luganville, Isangel, Lakatoro.
- [GeoNames: Samoa populated places](https://www.geonames.org/WS/largest-cities-in-samoa.html) — Apia and Asau.
- [Samoa Bureau of Statistics: 2021 census](https://www.sbs.gov.ws/documents/census/2021/Census_2021_Final_Report.pdf) — village names including Safotu and Asau.
- [GeoNames: Tonga populated places](https://www.geonames.org/TO/largest-cities-in-tonga.html) — Nuku'alofa, Neiafu, Haveluloto and Vaini.
- [Tonga census 2011, government report hosted by DFAT](https://www.dfat.gov.au/sites/default/files/tonga-census-preliminary-result-2011.pdf) — Haveluloto and Vaini villages.

The existing polygons remain attributed to Natural Earth (public domain) through `map-data-notes.json`; this task did not change polygons.
